About Red Rocks Exotic Reptiles

We are a Colorado reptile business built by keepers who prioritize clean husbandry, responsible breeding, honest education, and long-term support.

Our reptile business

Red Rocks Exotic Reptiles began as a reptile-keeping hobby and grew into a business in 2023. We work with geckos, skinks, arboreal lizards, snakes, feeder insects, aquatic plants, and custom husbandry products.

We are proud to be a woman-, veteran-, Hispanic-, and Native American-owned company. Our goal is to help customers choose appropriate animals and supplies while promoting responsible care.

What customers can expect

  • Ethically bred and responsibly sourced animals
  • Transparent information about availability and care requirements
  • Practical husbandry support before and after a purchase
  • Secure online checkout for eligible products
  • Weather-conscious live-animal shipping and Colorado pickup options
