Shipping, Returns & Terms

Last updated: July 23, 2026
By placing an order with Red Rocks Exotic Reptiles LLC, you agree to the policies that apply to the products in that order. Online checkout currently applies to eligible dry goods only unless a listing explicitly states otherwise.

Online payment

Online payments are processed securely by Stripe. Prices are shown in United States dollars. Applicable shipping charges and taxes are displayed before payment is submitted. An order is not accepted until payment succeeds and an order confirmation is issued.

Dry-goods processing and shipping

  • Orders normally require one to three business days for processing before carrier acceptance.
  • Transit estimates shown during checkout are estimates and are not delivery guarantees.
  • Customers are responsible for providing a complete, accurate, and deliverable shipping address.
  • Risk of loss transfers after confirmed delivery, except where applicable law requires otherwise.
  • We may contact the customer, delay fulfillment, or cancel and refund an order when an address, inventory, pricing, fraud, safety, or shipping issue prevents reasonable fulfillment.

Dry-goods cancellations, damage, and returns

Dry-goods sales are final unless the item arrives damaged, the wrong item was sent, we approve a cancellation before fulfillment, or a refund or replacement is otherwise required by law.

  • Contact us as soon as possible to request cancellation. Orders already packed, shipped, or submitted for carrier pickup may not be cancellable.
  • Report damaged, defective, missing, or incorrect items within three calendar days of delivery. Include the order number and clear photographs of the item and packaging.
  • Do not return merchandise without written authorization. Unauthorized returns may be refused.
  • Food, supplements, feeders, plants, custom items, made-to-order items, and opened or used products are not returnable unless damaged, defective, incorrect, or required by law.
  • When a discretionary return is approved, original shipping charges are not refundable and the customer may be responsible for return shipping.

Live animals and other living products

Live animals are not sold through the standard dry-goods Stripe checkout unless a listing expressly says otherwise. Live-animal purchases are arranged directly or through an approved marketplace and are subject to the specific terms provided for that sale.

  • The customer must confirm that possession and delivery of the species is legal at the destination.
  • The customer must be available to receive and inspect the shipment promptly.
  • Any arrival concern must be reported immediately with photographs and the applicable order or shipment information.
  • Once a live animal is confirmed delivered in acceptable condition, responsibility for its care, housing, and welfare transfers to the customer.
