Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 23, 2026

Red Rocks Exotic Reptiles LLC respects your privacy. This policy explains how information is handled through rrereptiles.com, our online store, customer communications, and the ReptiLog app.

Information collected for online orders

When you place or attempt to place an online order, we may receive and store:

  • Your name, email address, telephone number, and shipping address.
  • Products ordered, quantities, prices, shipping charges, taxes, order status, and fulfillment records.
  • Transaction identifiers and payment status supplied by Stripe.
  • Technical information needed for security and reliable operation, such as request timestamps and error records.

Red Rocks Exotic Reptiles does not receive or store your complete card number. Payment details are collected and processed by Stripe through its secure checkout interface.

How order information is used

Order information is used to calculate shipping and applicable taxes, process payment, fulfill and support orders, prevent fraud, maintain inventory and accounting records, comply with legal obligations, and communicate about your purchase.

Service providers

  • Stripe processes payments and calculates applicable transaction taxes.
  • Supabase hosts storefront order, inventory, and application data.
  • USPS provides shipping-rate and delivery services.
  • Resend may deliver transactional order emails.
  • Cloudflare and GitHub support website hosting and deployment.

These providers receive only the information reasonably needed to provide their services and operate under their own privacy and security terms.

ReptiLog data

ReptiLog can be used offline without an account. When used entirely offline, husbandry data remains on the device. Customers who create an account for cloud features provide an email address and may sync animal, group, log, and settings data through Supabase.

Data sharing and advertising

We do not sell personal information. We do not provide customer order information to advertisers. Information may be disclosed when required by law, to protect the security of our systems and customers, or to providers working on our behalf.

Retention and security

We retain order and transaction records for as long as reasonably necessary for fulfillment, customer service, accounting, tax, fraud-prevention, and legal purposes. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, but no online system can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we control. Some transaction records may need to be retained for legal, tax, accounting, dispute, or fraud-prevention purposes.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our services or legal obligations change. The current version and update date will remain available on this page.
