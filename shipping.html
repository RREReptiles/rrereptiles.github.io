Shipping, Checkout & Colorado Pickup

How Red Rocks Exotic Reptiles handles secure checkout, shippable products, live animals, feeder insects, and local pickup.

Secure checkout

Eligible products use Stripe for secure card and supported wallet payments. Shipping and applicable sales tax are calculated from the delivery address during checkout.

Live-animal shipping

Eligible live animals are shipped using appropriate insulated packaging and temperature controls. Weather, carrier service, and safe receiving conditions may require a shipping hold or coordinated delivery date.

Live-arrival concerns

Inspect live shipments promptly. Contact us immediately with clear photos and order details if there is a concern on arrival so the situation can be documented and reviewed.

Feeder insects

Live feeder insects and cultures are Colorado local-pickup items. They are not offered through standard parcel shipping.

Local and show pickup

Pickup is available only by prior arrangement. We also offer reserved pickup at eligible Colorado reptile shows where we are vending.

Questions before ordering

Contact us with the item name and destination ZIP code for shipping or availability questions.
