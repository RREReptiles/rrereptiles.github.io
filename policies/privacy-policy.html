Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 24, 2026

Red Rocks Exotic Reptiles LLC respects your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect information through rrereptiles.com, our online store, and related customer communications. ReptiLog is covered by its own privacy policy.

Information we collect

Depending on how you use the website, we may collect:

  • Contact and order information: your name, email address, telephone number, billing or shipping address, products ordered, quantities, prices, shipping charges, taxes, order status, and messages you send us.
  • Payment and transaction information: transaction identifiers, payment status, payment method type, and limited payment details supplied by Stripe. Red Rocks Exotic Reptiles does not receive or store your complete card number or security code.
  • Technical information: information reasonably needed to secure and operate the website, such as IP address, browser or device information, request timestamps, and error or security logs.
  • Browser storage: the storefront uses local storage to remember cart contents and session storage to maintain an active checkout session. These records are used for storefront operation, not behavioral advertising.

How we use information

We use information to operate the website, process and fulfill orders, calculate shipping and applicable taxes, process payments and refunds, communicate about purchases, provide customer service, maintain inventory and accounting records, prevent fraud or misuse, improve reliability, and comply with legal obligations.

Payments

Online payments are processed by Stripe through its secure checkout interface. Stripe may collect payment, identity, device, transaction, and fraud-prevention information under its own privacy terms. We receive the order and transaction information needed to complete and support your purchase, but we do not store complete card credentials.

Service providers and disclosures

We may share information with service providers only as reasonably necessary to operate the website and fulfill orders, including:

  • Stripe for checkout, payment processing, fraud prevention, refunds, and applicable transaction taxes.
  • Supabase for storefront, order, inventory, and application data hosting.
  • USPS, UPS, FedEx, and other shipping providers for rates, labels, delivery, tracking, and claims.
  • Resend or other email providers for transactional order communications.
  • GitHub Pages, Cloudflare, and related infrastructure providers for website hosting, delivery, security, and deployment.

We may also disclose information when required by law, to respond to valid legal process, protect customers or our business, investigate fraud or security incidents, or complete a business transfer.

Advertising and sale of information

We do not sell personal information. We do not provide customer order information to advertisers, and we do not currently use the website for targeted advertising or third-party behavioral analytics.

Retention and security

We retain order and transaction records for as long as reasonably necessary for fulfillment, customer service, accounting, tax, dispute resolution, fraud prevention, and legal compliance. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, but no online system can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we control. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request. Certain transaction records may be retained when required for legal, tax, accounting, dispute, or fraud-prevention purposes.

You may remove storefront cart information by clearing your browser storage. Browser settings may also be used to restrict local or session storage, although doing so can prevent the cart or checkout from working correctly.

Children's privacy

The website and online store are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. A parent or guardian who believes a child has provided personal information may contact us to request its deletion.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our storefront, service providers, or legal obligations change. The current version and its last-updated date will remain available on this page.
